\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

Sengoku concentrates its visual identity in the front matter while keeping the body organized through familiar sections, equations, tables, and references. The same class can therefore support a visually expressive project report or a simpler manuscript with decorative elements omitted. Authors can retain the shared typography while choosing which logos and resource rows are useful for their readers.

The template is intended for preprints and technical reports. A conference or journal may require its own document class, page dimensions, or author format; those requirements should guide the submitted version. Long titles and large author lists also benefit from a final visual check. Short running titles, adjustable logo sizes, and alternative affiliation layouts provide practical ways to adapt the design to the manuscript.
