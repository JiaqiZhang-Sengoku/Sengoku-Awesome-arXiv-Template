\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

A research manuscript often connects a written contribution with code, datasets, models, and other supporting materials. Sengoku is a customizable \LaTeX{} template that brings these elements into a consistent document design for preprints and technical reports. Its front matter gives readers a concise view of the paper's identity and available resources before the main text begins.

The template combines a colored title card and a four-color title with flexible logo placement, author and affiliation blocks, and optional resource links. These elements can be configured for an individual author, a collaboration, or an institutional project. Figure~\ref{fig:sengoku-overview} presents this idea through a promotional illustration of manuscript preparation, configurable layout, and project resources.

Manuscript text lives in separate section files, while the entry file manages paper information and the class defines the layout.
