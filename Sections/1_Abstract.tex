\begin{abstract}
Sengoku is a customizable \LaTeX{} template for preprints and technical reports. Its design combines a colored title card, a multicolor title, flexible logo placement, and configurable author and affiliation blocks. Optional links to code, datasets, models, and other project resources are presented alongside the paper information in the front matter. Title, author, affiliation, and resource settings are managed in a single entry file, while manuscript sections remain separate from the document class, making the template easy to adapt to different papers, institutions, and projects.
\end{abstract}
